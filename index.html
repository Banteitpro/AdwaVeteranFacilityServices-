Meet the Founder

KALEB MEKONNEN

Founder & Managing Member

ADWA Veteran Facility Services was founded with a commitment to dependable service, thoughtful attention to detail, and personal accountability. Drawing on his U.S. Army experience, the founder brings a practical, organized approach to leadership and a strong respect for the trust clients place in the company.

His approach centers on clear communication, thorough planning, and consistent follow-through. From the initial inquiry to the completion of each cleaning, the goal is to make working with ADWA straightforward and responsive to each client’s needs.

Beginning with residential cleaning, ADWA is building a foundation for future growth in commercial cleaning, government facility cleaning, and facility support throughout Pennsylvania and Southern New Jersey. Service plans are developed around each property’s requirements, the client’s priorities, and a clearly defined scope of work.

The founder believes lasting client relationships are earned through reliable performance and honest communication. Through direct owner involvement, careful coordination, and attention to feedback, he is developing ADWA into a trusted partner for the homes, businesses, and communities it serves.
