        Meet the founder

        Kaleb Mekonnen

        Founder & Managing Member · U.S. Army Staff Sergeant Veteran

        Kaleb Mekonnen founded ADWA Veteran Facility Services to bring military discipline, accountability, and attention to detail to the commercial cleaning and facility-services industry. His honorable service in the U.S. Army, including leadership experience as a Staff Sergeant, strengthened his ability to lead teams, manage responsibilities, maintain high standards, and complete every mission with professionalism.

        Beyond his military service, Kaleb brings a disciplined, standards-driven approach to running the business. He believes in thorough documentation, dependable execution, responsive customer service, and measurable performance — principles that guide how ADWA plans, performs, inspects, and continuously improves its services on every account.

        As a service-disabled veteran-owned small business, ADWA Veteran Facility Services will provide dependable janitorial, commercial cleaning, and facility support services to offices, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, property-management companies, nonprofit organizations, and government agencies throughout Pennsylvania and Southern New Jersey. Planned services include recurring janitorial cleaning, restroom sanitation, floor and carpet care, high-touch surface disinfection, move-in and move-out cleaning, post-construction cleaning, pressure washing, and customized facility-support programs.

        Kaleb’s goal is to build more than a cleaning company. He is developing a trusted facility-services partner that delivers consistent quality, responsive communication, trained personnel, and direct owner accountability. Every facility will be carefully assessed before a proposal is prepared, and every client will receive a service plan designed around its schedule, operational requirements, and expectations.

        The name

        Adwa is the Ethiopian town where, in 1896, an outnumbered force turned back a colonial army — a victory that has stood for self-determination ever since.

        The crowned lion in our mark comes from that history. It is the standard the company is named for.
