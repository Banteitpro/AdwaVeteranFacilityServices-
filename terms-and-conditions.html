SMS Messaging Program

Customers may voluntarily opt in to receive text messages from ADWA Veteran Facility Services LLC. Messages may include responses to cleaning-service inquiries, quote and estimate coordination, appointment scheduling and reminders, service-status updates, customer-support responses, and post-service follow-up.

Message frequency varies depending on the customer’s requests and service activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Customers may opt out at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, the customer will receive a confirmation message and will receive no further text messages unless the customer opts in again. Customers may reply HELP for assistance or call +1 (888) 983-9843.

Wireless carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to the customer’s wireless service and network availability.

Customers must provide a valid mobile number and have authority to consent to receiving messages at that number.
